# P29_INEQ25 S6: implementation deviations and clarifications (card frozen at 17a056d)

Each entry gives the reason and whether results informed it. None changes a verdict rule, margin, family or the central estimator.

**D1. Source of the 1825 cross-section for S-d and S-e (results informed: no).** The 1825 district data (`all_districts`) are taken from the S5 re-run of the patched original script (`compare/_scratch/obj/all_districts.rds`, read-only, md5 logged in `output/01_data_log.txt`) instead of re-implementing the 1,200-line cleaning block (l.1286-2500). They feed only the secondary outputs S-d (1825 ownership shares) and S-e (Swellendam settlers). Everything that bears on H1-H3 is rebuilt from the raw workbooks in `01_data.R`, and the rebuilt opgaaf panel is verified identical to the code's `opgaaf_save2`.

**D2. Bethelsdorp wheat does not enter H3b (results informed: no).** The card says an asset enters H3b "if its column exists in that sheet". The 1814 `Bethelsdorp` sheet has a `wheat_reaped` column, but all 425 entries are blank, and the card's variable mappings (l.2683-2717) define no Bethelsdorp wheat. An all-blank column has no Gini. It is therefore left out, which is decided by the data's structure (seen before any Gini was computed), not by results.

**D3. Swartrivier sheep has no recorded holders (results informed: the fact was discovered when the central H3b point estimate came back undefined).** The card adds Swartrivier sheep (wethers + breeding) to H3b as a correction. In `Swartrivier.xlsx` every one of the 364 entries in both sheep columns is ".", so the constructed variable is zero for every household and its Gini is 0/0. The node is therefore not computable, and H3b is not-testable under §8 rule 0 (it is also not-testable because only 0 of 9,999 draws are jointly valid, as the card anticipated in §4 and §10). No rule was changed. Two consequences for report-only analyses: (i) S-h excludes it automatically (0 holders < 10); (ii) the S-h zero-replicate convention can only replace undefined *draws*, not an undefined point estimate, so that analysis is run over the 12 H3b assets with a computable point. The 2026 audit item 4 ("Swartrivier sheep omitted") is thus without effect: there were no sheep to omit.

**D4. V6 trimming and synthetic rows (results informed: no).** The card drops a household row from the basket when any component exceeds 10 x the year's 99th percentile of positive values. For H2 the synthetic zero rows of a dropped owner row are dropped with it, since they are generated from that row. One row is flagged.

**D5. S-f applies the date-like rule to the eight named values only (results informed: no).** The card says "the eight date-like counts set to NA" (S5-15: six `horse_nr`, two `wagon_nr`). `compare/s5_checks/s5_02_checks.R` applied its year-like rule to all five count columns, which also blanked three `sheep_nr` values (1700, 1796, 1800 sheep) that look like genuine flock sizes. The modern script restricts the rule to `horse_nr` and `wagon_nr` and asserts that exactly eight values change.

**D6. Confidence level of secondary outputs (results informed: no).** The card fixes 98.75% for the family {H1, H2, H3a, H3b} and is silent on the secondary outputs S-a to S-g. These are reported with 95% percentile intervals (they are outside the family and never verdicts). The 5C.3 diagnostics and S-h mirror the verdict machinery and use 98.75%.

**D7. Missingness alternatives use the code's post-cleaning NAs (results informed: no).** Alternatives (a) and (b) of §5C.1 are defined on the NAs that remain after the code's own year-level NA->0 rule, i.e. the NAs the basket's NA->0 convention zeroes. Raw-blank shares (no entry in any source column) are reported alongside in `missingness.csv`. Under this definition (a) drops no row, and (b) drops 5,869 rows, including all of decade 1690.

No package was installed. Runtime: about 2 minutes for 03 and under 1 minute for 04 on 30 workers, so no replication count was halved. The MC SE rule did not trigger escalation to 39,999 (largest verdict-relevant MC SE 0.0007).
